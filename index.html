Who we are

Where knowledge meets public purpose.

The Centre for Research & Policy Studies (CRP) is envisioned as a multidisciplinary platform for research, policy dialogue and capacity development.

Our focus is on bringing together researchers, practitioners and institutions to examine real-world challenges and develop practical, inclusive and evidence-informed recommendations.

From education access and social opportunity to economic resilience and sustainability, our work is guided by intellectual integrity and measurable public value.

Our foundations

Vision, mission & values

Our Vision

To contribute to a more equitable, informed and sustainable society through credible interdisciplinary research and responsible policy dialogue.

Our Mission

To generate useful evidence, strengthen research skills, convene constructive dialogue and support the translation of knowledge into policy and practice.

Our Values

Integrity, independence, inclusivity, methodological rigour, collaboration, transparency and public benefit.

Proposed organisational structure

Director / Research Lead · Research Fellows and Associates · Programme Manager · Policy & Partnerships Coordinator · Advisory Panel. Appointments and affiliations will be published only when confirmed.
